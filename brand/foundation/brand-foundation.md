# RE-COMP Brand Foundation

Status: working foundation  
Owner: Branding chat  
Last reviewed: 2026-07-19

## Role

RE-COMP is a Health Operating System for health, performance, recovery, nutrition and guidance.

It helps people understand their current body state and decide what to do next.

## Core Promise

RE-COMP turns body signals into daily guidance.

Every important product and communication surface should move from:

1. Current state
2. Reason
3. Suggested action

## Strategic Territory

RE-COMP lives between fitness, wellness and medical prevention.

It should feel advanced, personal and actionable without becoming cold, clinical or generic.

Reference territory:

- Apple: clarity, restraint, native-feeling behavior
- Bevel: health intelligence and biometric depth
- Whoop: performance and recovery seriousness
- Superpower: medical wellness and longevity orientation
- Nike: human performance energy

These are references, not templates.

## Brand Attributes

RE-COMP should feel:

- precise
- premium
- calm
- intelligent
- credible
- health-first
- performance-oriented
- clinical without being sterile
- Apple-adjacent in care and behavior, not an Apple clone

RE-COMP should not feel:

- generic fitness
- gym brand
- motivational sports poster
- pure medical app
- dashboard full of disconnected numbers
- cyberpunk
- neon health tech
- SaaS template
- AI hype machine

## Product Belief

Tracking is not enough.

Health data becomes useful when it changes what the user understands and what they do next.

## Product Loop

Signals  
to  
Understanding  
to  
Guidance  
to  
Daily Action

## Brand Decision Rule

When choosing between two directions, prefer the one that is:

- clearer
- calmer
- more legible
- more useful
- less decorative
- more durable

## Current Assumptions

- RE-COMP is consumer-facing first, with enough credibility to support expert or clinical-adjacent contexts later.
- The brand may use dark cinematic atmosphere in marketing while the app remains light-first for repeated daily use.
- No health or medical claim should be made unless supported by future product, clinical or regulatory validation.
