# RE-COMP Imagery System

Status: draft foundation  
Source references: generated asset log and social design system

## Principle

Imagery should make health intelligence feel real, embodied and calm.

RE-COMP imagery is not generic wellness, not gym motivation and not futuristic diagnostics.

## Preferred Image Worlds

Use:

- human movement with restraint
- recovery in low light
- quiet readiness and fatigue moments
- hands holding a phone with RE-COMP UI
- clinical/longevity environments without diagnosis cues
- premium sports motion with cinematic light
- soft graphite, cool gray and warm skin tones
- real texture, breath, posture, strain and rest

Avoid:

- bodybuilding or gym-stock posing
- fake futuristic labs
- neon biohacking
- smiling stock wellness scenes
- medical imagery that implies diagnosis
- exaggerated sweat aesthetics
- over-polished AI faces
- decorative UI floating without purpose

## Composition Rules

Use:

- full-bleed imagery for brand and social
- strong negative space for type
- one dominant subject or decision
- restrained UI overlays as evidence
- subtle grain or atmospheric depth when appropriate

Avoid:

- centered SaaS cards on gradients
- overloaded metric dashboards
- busy transparency
- generic icons over photos
- decorative blobs or orbs

## UI Overlay Rule

When health data appears on imagery, it must provide meaning.

Good overlay structure:

- one score or signal
- one interpretation
- one recommended action

Do not show raw data as decoration.
