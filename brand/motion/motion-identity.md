# RE-COMP Motion Identity

Status: draft foundation  
Related: `brand/motion/logo-motion.md`, `TOKENS.md`

## Principle

Motion explains relationships.

It should help the user understand where something came from, what changed and what action is available.

Motion should not perform for its own sake.

## Character

RE-COMP motion should feel:

- precise
- calm
- responsive
- spatial
- lightly physical
- never elastic for spectacle

Avoid:

- endless glow loops
- decorative shimmer
- heavy blur
- playful bounce
- aggressive sports edits
- cyberpunk scanning effects

## Timing Direction

Working timings:

- 100-150ms: tap and tiny feedback
- 250ms: controls, hover, focus
- 400ms: panel entrance and card reveal
- 650ms max: major transitions

Preferred easing:

- ease-out
- cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)
- precise high-damping spring

## Brand Applications

Allowed:

- subtle logo reveal
- spatial panel transitions
- tab and navigation continuity
- sheet entry that clarifies hierarchy
- shared element movement where it improves comprehension
- restrained highlight on a container, not on the core mark

## Accessibility

All motion systems must support reduced motion.

Reduced motion should preserve meaning through opacity, position and hierarchy changes without removing essential feedback.

## Open Decisions

- No final app-wide motion examples are approved.
- Logo motion is documented but not production-tested.
- Social and reel motion templates are not yet defined.
