# RE-COMP Brand Guidelines

Status: working brand system

## Principle

RE-COMP should feel like a calm health operating system: clinical, premium, minimal and alive.

The brand mark stays stable. Context, interface state and motion may change around it.

## Brand Core

RE-COMP turns body signals into daily guidance.

Every important product or communication surface should help the user understand:

1. What is my current state?
2. Why does it matter?
3. What should I do next?

The brand should make health feel advanced, personal and actionable without becoming cold, generic or overclaiming.

## Current Logo Direction

Approved direction to explore:

- continuous-line R
- monoline geometry
- rounded terminals
- dark neutral glass container as UI context, not as mandatory logo content
- restrained wordmark with wide tracking

The current PNG references are concept assets, not production masters.

Required variants:

- favicon-optimized version

The glass container must remain separate from the core mark whenever possible.

## UI Consistency

Landing, app mockups and brand components must share the same visual values:

- dark neutral surfaces when immersive
- light-first stable surfaces for daily app content
- solid readable content cards
- Liquid Glass only for controls, navigation, tabs, segmented controls and transient action surfaces
- compact capsule controls
- restrained green, blue and violet accents
- no generic SaaS gradients
- no decorative glass applied to every card

## Experience Split

The brand may use cinematic dark atmosphere in landing, social and diagnostic moments.

The app core remains light-first, calm and readable unless a specific screen requires a diagnostic or immersive mode.

## Temporary Usage

It is acceptable to use PNG logo concepts in the current landing demo.

They must be treated as temporary visual assets until the vector reconstruction is complete.
