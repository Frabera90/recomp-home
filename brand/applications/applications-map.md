# RE-COMP Brand Applications Map

Status: working map

## Purpose

This document defines how the brand should translate across major surfaces without letting any one prototype redefine the whole identity.

## App

Role: daily operational health guidance.

Should feel:

- light-first
- native-aware
- calm
- readable
- action-oriented

Brand behaviors:

- every major surface should produce insight, recommendation and action
- dense content stays stable and legible
- glass is reserved for controls and transient surfaces
- diagnostic moments may use dark graphite or soft metal

Current implementation reference:

- `experiments/app-ui-pilot/`

Status:

- provisional pilot, not final architecture

## Landing

Role: narrative and positioning surface.

Should feel:

- cinematic
- premium
- confident
- health-first
- more expressive than the app

Brand behaviors:

- may use full-bleed imagery
- may use dark atmospheric moments
- should not invent product behavior
- should not redefine app layout rules

Current implementation reference:

- `recomp-landing-prototype/`

## Social

Role: premium health intelligence publication.

Should feel:

- editorial
- human
- data-aware
- cinematic
- restrained

Reference:

- `05_marketing/social/social-design-system.md`

Status:

- draft foundation

## Presentations

Role: communicate strategy, product logic and brand credibility.

Should feel:

- concise
- premium
- structured
- evidence-led

Open need:

- presentation-specific templates and rules.

## Generated Images

Role: explore and support brand, social, site and product mockup imagery.

Rules:

- use the asset log
- mark status clearly
- do not overwrite assets from another area without explicit request
- do not imply unsupported medical claims
