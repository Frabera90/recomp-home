# RE-COMP Color And Material System

Status: working direction  
Source references: `VISUAL_LANGUAGE.md`, `TOKENS.md`, `design-system/tokens.css`

## Core Principle

Color in RE-COMP is functional before expressive.

The interface is primarily neutral. Accents identify meaning, modules and state. They do not decorate.

## Experience Split

RE-COMP currently has two valid visual modes:

- App core: light-first, readable, calm and operational.
- Brand atmosphere: dark, cinematic, soft-metal and premium.

These modes are complementary.

The app should use dark/soft-metal moments for diagnostic, high-value or immersive surfaces. It should not turn every dashboard, report or list into glass or dark metal.

## Neutral Foundation

Use:

- white
- off-white
- very light cool gray
- graphite
- near-black

Avoid:

- pure black as a flat background
- beige wellness palettes
- loud fitness color systems
- decorative gradients
- rainbow dashboards

## Accent Roles

Module accents:

- Recovery: violet / purple
- Nutrition: health green
- Biomarkers: cool blue
- Coaching: warm gold

Accent rules:

- Use one dominant accent at a time.
- Use accents to explain state, category or action.
- Do not recolor the logo per module.
- Do not fill large surfaces with saturated accent color.
- Do not use accent color as visual excitement.

## Materials

### Health Paper

Role: default app content.

Use for:

- daily overview
- lists
- plans
- reports
- recommendations
- readable explanatory text

Behavior:

- light
- stable
- low transparency
- high contrast
- minimal visual noise

### Soft Metal

Role: premium diagnostic and immersive surfaces.

Use for:

- high-value insight moments
- recovery detail
- biometric interpretation
- landing and social atmosphere
- brand boards

Behavior:

- dark graphite or near-black
- subtly reflective
- micro gradient
- micro texture
- controlled light

### Functional Glass

Role: interaction material.

Use for:

- navigation
- tab bars
- floating actions
- filters
- search
- AI Coach composer
- sheets
- popovers
- overlays

Do not use for:

- dense data
- long text
- biomarker tables
- every card
- backgrounds
- decorative sections

## Freeze Candidate

Freeze this rule after review:

The brand may be cinematic and dark in expression, but the app core remains light-first unless a specific screen needs diagnostic focus.
