# Generated Assets

This folder stores generated or edited visual assets for RE-COMP.

Use the subfolders to keep image work separate from product code and from source/reference material.

## Folders

- `social/`: social posts, ads, campaign imagery and visuals briefed from `05_marketing/social/`.
- `backgrounds/`: backgrounds, textures and atmosphere images.
- `mockups/`: device mockups, product scenes and deck mockups.
- `ui-images/`: UI-specific visual assets and app concept images.
- `site-images/`: landing and website imagery.
- `brand/`: brand-adjacent visual explorations.
- `archive/`: unused, old or superseded generated assets.

## Log

Track generated assets in `ASSET_LOG.md`.

Do not treat generated assets as final product evidence unless they are validated and marked selected or frozen.

Social images should be tied back to a social brief or post entry in `05_marketing/social/`.
