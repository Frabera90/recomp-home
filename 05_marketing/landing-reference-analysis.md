## Main Direction

The landing page should be treated as a separate experience from the app UI.

It should share the same positioning and quality bar, but its job is different:

- explain the vision
- build trust
- show product value
- create desire
- convert visitors

## Reference Pattern 1: Bevel-Like Hero

Useful elements:

- centered promise
- immediate product preview
- wearable/app ecosystem signal
- soft atmospheric background
- rating/social proof
- download CTA

Why it matters:

This pattern is strong for a product-led app landing page.

## Reference Pattern 2: Superpower-Like Membership Section

Useful elements:

- large real-world photo
- product UI placed inside the physical scene
- numbered narrative steps
- FAQ overlays
- clinical credibility
- premium membership framing

Why it matters:

This pattern is stronger for the Health OS and care positioning.

## Landing Opportunity For RE-COMP

RE-COMP should combine:

- Bevel's direct app clarity
- Superpower's medical-wellness credibility
- Nike's human performance energy
- Apple's restraint and product confidence

## Recommended Landing Tone

The landing should feel:

- premium
- precise
- human
- health-first
- performance-aware
- less gym, more body intelligence

## Recommended First Draft Structure

1. Hero: Health OS promise, product visual, primary CTA
2. Health state section: show score, biomarkers, recovery and nutrition in one system
3. How it works: assess, interpret, guide, improve
4. Product modules: recovery, nutrition, coaching, biomarkers, AI
5. Coach/professional layer
6. Trust and credibility
7. Final CTA

## Separation From App UI

The landing can use:

- larger imagery
- scroll-based reveals
- cinematic product compositions
- storytelling sections
- motion graphics

The app should remain:

- operational
- readable
- task-oriented
- dense but calm
