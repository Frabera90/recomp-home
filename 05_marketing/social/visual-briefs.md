## Visual Rules

- Follow `VISUAL_LANGUAGE.md`.
- Use Soft Metal as dominant surface language.
- Use Liquid Glass for interaction or overlay moments only.
- Use Light as material: soft reflections, edge highlights, ambient light.
- Avoid neon, cyberpunk, gaming and generic fitness imagery.
- Avoid unsupported medical implications.
