# RE-COMP Social

## Purpose

This area owns RE-COMP social strategy, content formats, post concepts, captions and publishing-ready social assets.

It is connected to generated images, but it is not an image dump.

## Source Of Truth

Before creating social work, read:

- `VISUAL_LANGUAGE.md`
- `RECOMP_MASTER.md`
- `brand/brand-guidelines.md`
- `design-system/tokens.css`
- `design-system/components.md`
- `02_context/product.md`

- `05_marketing/social/strategy.md`
- `05_marketing/social/content-pillars.md`
- `05_marketing/social/format-system.md`
- `06_assets/generated/ASSET_LOG.md`

## Image & Mockup Lab Connection

The Social chat can use images generated by the Image & Mockup Lab.

Before requesting or generating a new image:

1. Check `06_assets/generated/ASSET_LOG.md`.
2. Check existing generated folders, especially `social/`, `site-images/`, `mockups/`, `ui-images/`, `backgrounds/` and `brand/`.
3. Reuse suitable existing images when possible.
4. Record the new social usage in the relevant post, brief or calendar entry.

Do not overwrite, move or rename generated assets created for another area unless the user explicitly asks.

## Brand And UI Access

Social should use the same RE-COMP visual vocabulary as app and landing:

- buttons and controls from current app/design-system references
- module colors from `design-system/tokens.css`
- logo rules from `brand/`
- Soft Metal, Liquid Glass and Light rules from `VISUAL_LANGUAGE.md`

Social must not invent new button styles, logo treatments or color systems.

## Social Work Types

- Strategy: positioning, audience, editorial principles.
- Content Pillars: recurring ideas and themes.
- Formats: post structures, carousel systems, reels/story directions.
- Campaigns: launch arcs and grouped content.
- Captions: reusable social copy.
- Visual Briefs: prompts and art direction for generated images.
- Calendar: planned posts and publishing notes.

## Rules

- Do not invent medical claims.
- Do not use generic fitness hype.
- Do not make RE-COMP look like a gym brand.
- Do not post raw health data without meaning.
- Every post should communicate insight, guidance or product intelligence.
- Social visuals must follow `VISUAL_LANGUAGE.md`.

## Workflow

1. Define the post objective.
2. Choose a content pillar.
3. Choose a format.
4. Write the caption/copy.
5. Create or select visual assets.
6. Log assets and status.
7. Mark the post as draft, selected, scheduled, published or archived.
