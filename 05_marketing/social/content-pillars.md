# Social Content Pillars

## 1. Health OS

Purpose:

Explain the category.

Examples:

- What a Health OS means.
- Why tracking is not enough.
- How signals become guidance.

## 2. Body Signals

Purpose:

Show how recovery, sleep, HRV, biomarkers, nutrition and training connect.

Examples:

- One signal does not tell the whole story.
- Recovery changes the plan.
- Biomarkers need interpretation.

## 3. Daily Guidance

Purpose:

Make RE-COMP's value tangible.

Examples:

- "How am I today?"
- "Why?"
- "What should I do next?"

## 4. Performance As Care

Purpose:

Position training, nutrition and recovery as long-term health behavior.

Examples:

- Performance without burnout.
- Recovery as a training input.
- Nutrition as adherence support.

## 5. Product Intelligence

Purpose:

Show interface, system logic and AI/expert guidance.

Examples:

- Health score interpretation.
- AI Coach grounded in context.
- Plans adapting to state.

## 6. Brand And Visual Language

Purpose:

Build premium perception.

Examples:

- Material studies.
- Light, depth and glass studies.
- App mockup reveals.

## 7. Proof And Trust

Purpose:

Show credibility without unsupported medical claims.

Examples:

- Expert workflow.
- Body Check as baseline.
- Coaching layer.
- Partner or community proof when validated.
