# Social Format System

## Static Post

Use for:

- single strong idea
- product visual
- quote/claim with restraint
- material or app detail

Structure:

- one message
- one visual focus
- one caption that adds context

## Carousel

Use for:

- education
- product logic
- before/after interpretation
- Health OS explanation

Suggested structure:

1. Hook
2. Problem
3. Signal/context
4. Interpretation
5. RE-COMP guidance
6. Takeaway

## Reel / Short Video

Use for:

- motion system
- app interaction
- material/light studies
- signal-to-insight sequence

Rules:

- calm motion
- no hype editing
- no aggressive music direction
- motion must explain the product

## Story

Use for:

- quick product teasers
- behind-the-scenes material studies
- polls/questions
- soft launch updates

## LinkedIn Thought Post

Use for:

- category creation
- founder/product point of view
- health intelligence positioning

Structure:

- sharp observation
- why it matters
- RE-COMP point of view
- restrained CTA or question

## Visual Brief

Use for:

- generated images
- mockups
- post backgrounds
- campaign visual systems

Required fields:

- content pillar
- format
- visual objective
- references
- image destination
- caption status
