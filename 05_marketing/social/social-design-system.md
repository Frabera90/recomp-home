# RE-COMP Social Design System

Status: draft foundation  
Date: 2026-07-18  
Scope: social-only visual direction, formats and composition rules

## Role

This document translates the RE-COMP visual language into social content.

It is informed by the current RE-COMP brand, design system, app UI primitives, landing visual direction and the provided reference set from Superpower, Bevel, Oura Ring and Everlab.

It does not redefine product UI, app architecture, landing design, brand masters or design system tokens.

## Social Position

RE-COMP social should feel like a premium health intelligence publication connected to a real product.

The style is:

- editorial
- calm
- clinical without feeling cold
- premium
- human
- data-aware
- cinematic
- restrained

It must not feel like:

- a gym brand
- motivational fitness content
- generic wellness advice
- cyberpunk health tech
- neon biohacking
- a SaaS dashboard ad

## Reference Extraction

The provided references share a clear system:

- real human photography or product-in-hand imagery
- large editorial typography placed directly over image
- one dominant message per post
- sparse UI overlays used as evidence, not decoration
- dark or muted image treatment
- soft blur, motion blur, grain or atmospheric depth
- health topics framed as questions, signals or decisions
- restrained microcopy below the headline
- small platform-native affordances kept unobtrusive
- minimal color, usually one accent at a time

For RE-COMP, this becomes:

Health data should enter the frame only when it helps the audience understand what the image means.

## Core Visual Formula

Every social asset should combine three layers:

1. Human or product moment
2. Editorial claim or question
3. Minimal RE-COMP intelligence layer

The intelligence layer can be:

- a score
- a signal card
- a recommendation
- a short data label
- a small UI crop
- a progress marker
- a single product module

It should never become a full dashboard unless the post is explicitly about product UI.

## Image Direction

Use photography and cinematic mockups that feel real, textured and close to the body.

Preferred subjects:

- recovery in low light
- sleep, fatigue and readiness
- hands holding phone with RE-COMP UI
- motion-blurred athletic movement
- skin, breath, posture, strain and rest
- body details cropped with restraint
- clinical/longevity environments
- quiet city or indoor wellness atmospheres
- product screens shown as part of daily life

Avoid:

- generic gym posing
- smiling stock wellness scenes
- fake futuristic labs
- neon diagnostics
- exaggerated sweat/bodybuilding aesthetics
- medical imagery that implies diagnosis
- over-polished AI faces

## Color And Atmosphere

Social visuals should use neutral, cinematic palettes:

- near-black graphite
- white and off-white type
- cool blue sleep/night scenes
- warm skin and amber light for human performance
- soft gray clinical backgrounds
- muted green, blue, violet or gold accents only when tied to modules

Use RE-COMP accents only as signal color:

- Recovery: violet
- Nutrition: green
- Biomarkers: blue
- Coaching: warm gold

Do not create rainbow systems or decorative gradients.

## Typography

Social typography may be more editorial than the app UI, but must remain precise and premium.

Primary behavior:

- large serif or refined editorial display headline for campaigns and thought posts
- clean sans-serif for labels, metrics, product captions and UI overlays
- strong scale contrast
- short lines
- generous spacing
- no negative letter spacing

Headline rules:

- one idea per frame
- maximum 2-4 short lines
- use questions when they create useful tension
- avoid hype verbs
- avoid medical certainty

Good headline shapes:

- "How ready are you, really?"
- "Tracking is not the same as knowing."
- "Six months of data. One clearer question."
- "Recovery changes the plan."
- "Your body signals are connected."

Avoid:

- "Unlock your full potential"
- "Crush your goals"
- "Biohack your body"
- "Transform your health"
- "Fix your hormones fast"

## Logo Use

Use the logo with restraint.

Allowed:

- small symbol in a corner
- small wordmark in campaign title frames
- logo inside a separate UI/glass container when used as product chrome

Rules:

- respect clear space
- do not recolor per module
- do not add glow, blur or shadow to the core mark
- do not force the logo onto busy/low-contrast media without a legibility container
- do not make the logo the main visual unless the post is about brand identity

## UI Overlay System

Social overlays should borrow from current RE-COMP primitives:

- compact rounded score cards
- glass controls for transient information
- soft metal surfaces for stable data
- small progress or range lines
- module chips
- recommendation strips
- phone UI crops

Overlay rules:

- one primary overlay per post
- keep it small enough to support the image
- align to the story in the photo
- use real product language where possible
- use state, meaning and action together when space allows

Example overlay patterns:

- Sleep score: 56, "Challenges falling asleep?"
- Health Score: 84, "Moderate strength recommended"
- HRV: 78 ms, "Improving"
- Recovery: 82%, "Good window for strength"
- Biomarkers: "Retest suggested in 8 weeks"

Never show raw data without interpretation.

## Layout Families

### 1. Editorial Image Post

Purpose: one strong concept or question.

Structure:

- full-bleed image
- headline over image
- short supporting line
- optional small logo
- optional one signal marker

Use for:

- category education
- body signals
- recovery/nutrition/biomarker concepts

### 2. Signal Overlay Post

Purpose: make data feel actionable.

Structure:

- full-bleed human or sports image
- one RE-COMP card overlay
- headline or question
- one metric and one interpretation

Use for:

- sleep score
- recovery readiness
- training load
- nutrition adherence

### 3. Product-In-Hand Post

Purpose: show RE-COMP as part of daily life.

Structure:

- hand-held phone or app screen in context
- short editorial headline
- minimal progress/timeline affordance
- caption expands the idea

Use for:

- Health OS explanation
- daily guidance
- product intelligence

### 4. Product UI Mockup Post

Purpose: make product logic tangible.

Structure:

- centered device or cropped UI
- dark calm background
- one explanatory sentence
- subtle dots/progress system for carousel/video

Use for:

- app feature reveals
- how RE-COMP interprets signals
- module-specific walkthroughs

### 5. Clinical Editorial Card

Purpose: explain environmental or biomarker context.

Structure:

- soft background image
- translucent panel
- small topical label
- inner image or visual proof
- headline and body
- restrained CTA

Use for:

- biomarkers
- air quality/stress/recovery context
- expert-led explanations

## Copy System

Social copy should sound like a calm expert, not a trainer.

Principles:

- make a sharp observation
- explain why it matters
- connect to RE-COMP's point of view
- avoid overpromising outcomes
- avoid unsupported medical claims
- prefer "may", "can", "helps", "suggests" when health causality is not validated

Default message:

RE-COMP turns body signals into daily guidance.

Supporting beliefs:

- Tracking is not enough.
- Interpretation is the product.
- Recovery, nutrition, biomarkers and training should not live in separate dashboards.
- Health data becomes useful only when it changes what you do next.
- Performance is not separate from care.

## Format-Specific Rules

### Instagram Static

- 4:5 vertical
- one headline
- one visual focus
- optional small UI overlay
- high contrast type
- caption carries context

### Instagram Carousel

- each frame must have one job
- first frame must be editorial and visual
- middle frames explain signal, context and interpretation
- final frame gives takeaway or product point of view

Recommended sequence:

1. Question or tension
2. Why tracking alone fails
3. Signal example
4. Interpretation
5. What RE-COMP would guide
6. Takeaway

### Reel / Short Video

- calm pacing
- cinematic motion or UI motion
- avoid aggressive edits
- use captions as editorial narration
- motion must clarify relationship between signal and action

### Story

- more direct and lightweight
- can use polls or questions
- keep the RE-COMP intelligence layer visible
- avoid clutter

### LinkedIn

- less image-led, more idea-led
- use product point of view
- avoid consumer hype
- strong opening observation
- restrained CTA

## Composition Rules

Use:

- full-bleed imagery
- large type integrated with subject
- text placed in natural negative space
- lower-third or center-weighted headline depending on image
- one small UI proof point
- cinematic crop
- grain or soft noise

Avoid:

- centered SaaS cards on gradients
- multiple stacked cards
- overloaded metric panels
- decorative blobs
- generic icons floating over photos
- dense paragraphs on image
- poster designs that could belong to any wellness app

`recomp-social-[pillar]-[format]-[concept]-v01-YYYYMMDD.png`

Examples:

`06_assets/generated/ASSET_LOG.md`

## Quality Bar

A RE-COMP social visual is successful when:

- the first read is immediate
- the image feels premium and real
- the data point has meaning
- the typography feels editorial, not template-based
- the UI overlay feels native to RE-COMP
- there is no unsupported health promise
- the asset could sit near Superpower, Bevel, Oura or Everlab without losing RE-COMP's identity
