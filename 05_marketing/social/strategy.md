# Social Strategy

## Role

Social should make RE-COMP feel like a premium Health OS before people ever open the product.

It should communicate:

- health intelligence
- performance with care
- recovery and longevity
- nutrition as guidance
- biomarkers as context
- expert/AI support

## Audience

Primary:

- people who train seriously but want health guidance, not hype
- people interested in longevity, prevention and performance
- premium wellness and health-conscious professionals

Secondary:

- coaches
- clinicians or specialists
- founders/operators evaluating RE-COMP as a serious platform

## Tone

Calm, precise, premium, intelligent.

Avoid:

- "crush your goals"
- gym motivation
- fear-based health messaging
- medical certainty
- cyberpunk visuals
- generic SaaS language

## Strategic Message

RE-COMP turns body signals into daily guidance.

It is not just tracking.

It is interpretation, recommendation and action.

## Social Objective

Build category understanding:

RE-COMP = Health OS for performance, recovery, nutrition and longevity.
