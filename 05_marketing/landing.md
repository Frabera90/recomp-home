# Landing Structure

## Hero

Understand your body.

Improve your future.

## Supporting Message

RE-COMP is a Health OS for performance, recovery, nutrition and longevity.

## Sections

- Health intelligence
- AI coaching
- Recovery system
- Performance tracking
- Nutrition guidance
- Medical integration

## Style

- minimal
- premium
- calm
- data-driven
- health-first
- performance-aware
- scroll-led
- product-visible
- cinematic but restrained

## Messaging Rule

The landing page should not feel like a generic fitness product.

It should communicate a more advanced system for understanding and improving the body.

## First Draft Direction

The first landing draft should combine:

- Bevel-like clarity and app confidence
- Superpower-like health credibility and membership framing
- Apple-like restraint
- Nike-like performance energy

The page should use motion to explain the product system, not just decorate the experience.
