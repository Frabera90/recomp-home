# Landing Motion Direction

## Why Motion Matters

The strongest references use motion and scroll behavior to make health intelligence feel alive.

For RE-COMP, motion should communicate:

- body signals becoming understandable
- fragmented data becoming one system
- AI interpretation
- progress over time
- premium product polish

## Motion Principles

- calm, not flashy
- informative, not decorative
- scroll-led, not random
- product-first, not abstract
- lightweight enough to perform well

## Recommended Scroll Effects

### Hero Product Reveal

As the user scrolls, the product mockup can move from a cinematic hero composition into a more structured UI view.

### Signal-To-Insight Sequence

Biomarkers, sleep, nutrition and recovery data appear as separate signals, then merge into a single health score or daily recommendation.

### Dark Glass Insight Reveal

Dark glass cards can fade and blur in only when the page introduces AI insight or deep analytics.

### Sticky Story Sections

Use pinned sections for:

- Assess
- Interpret
- Guide
- Improve

Each step can update the product visual on the right or center.

### Metrics Count-Up

Numbers should animate subtly when entering the viewport.

Avoid aggressive gamified counters.

## Tools And Libraries To Consider

For implementation:

- GSAP ScrollTrigger for precise scroll-based animation
- Framer Motion for React component animation
- Lenis for smooth scrolling
- Lottie or Rive for vector/motion graphics
- Three.js only if a real 3D product/biometric scene is needed

For design workflow:

- Figma for page direction and component structure
- Browser/Playwright verification for testing motion and responsive behavior
- Generated bitmap imagery only when custom hero/product visuals are needed

## Rule

Motion should make the product easier to understand.

It should not be used just to make the page feel expensive.
