# RE-COMP Landing Blueprint

## Goal

Create a first landing draft that communicates RE-COMP as a premium Health OS.

This is not a static design handoff.

The intended output is a live prototype that developers can inspect, browse and reuse as a code starting point.

## Strategic Direction

RE-COMP should combine:

- Bevel-like app clarity
- Superpower-like health credibility
- Everlab-like longevity and care positioning
- Apple-like restraint
- Nike-like performance energy

The old RE-COMP website should be used only as historical product context.

Preserve:

- Free Body Check
- coaching relationship
- nutrition and training modules
- professional/business layer
- RECOMP Wellness Plan
- premium longevity services in Italy

Reposition them under:

- health intelligence
- baseline assessment
- recovery and longevity
- daily guidance
- Health OS
- premium wellness journey

## Landing Promise

Understand your body.

Improve your future.

## Alternative Hero Lines

Option 1:

Your Health OS for performance, recovery and longevity.

Option 2:

Turn your body data into daily guidance.

Option 3:

Health intelligence for how you train, recover and live.

## Page Structure

### 1. Hero

Purpose:

Make the product category immediately clear.

Content:

- RE-COMP identity
- primary value proposition
- product mockup or animated health dashboard
- CTA
- credibility signal

Motion:

- subtle product reveal
- health score or data cards fade in
- background light movement

### 2. Data Becomes Guidance

Purpose:

Explain the shift from tracking to interpretation.

Content:

- biomarkers
- recovery
- nutrition
- training
- AI recommendation
- free body check as baseline entry point

Motion:

- separate signals animate into one recommendation card

### 3. How It Works

Purpose:

Make the system understandable.

Steps:

1. Assess
2. Interpret
3. Guide
4. Improve

Motion:

- sticky scroll section
- product visual changes as each step becomes active

### 4. Product Modules

Purpose:

Show the core Health OS areas.

Modules:

- Recovery
- Nutrition
- Biomarkers
- AI Coach
- Coaching Layer

Motion:

- cards reveal progressively
- selected module expands or highlights

### 5. Human Performance And Care

Purpose:

Position RE-COMP between fitness and medical wellness.

Content:

- health as prevention
- performance as care
- coaching and specialist support
- RECOMP Wellness Plan
- personalized longevity and wellness paths
- biomarker, movement, stress, metabolism and nutrition assessment

Visual:

- more editorial
- real human imagery or cinematic health/product composition

### 5B. RECOMP Wellness Plan

Purpose:

Introduce the premium service ecosystem.

Content:

- advanced longevity and wellness program
- personalized paths
- exclusive locations in Italy
- key biomarker screening
- specialist-supported longevity-oriented services
- functional movement and posture analysis
- nervous system and stress evaluation
- breathing, biofeedback and autonomic regulation
- metabolic and body composition analysis
- personalized nutrition and training strategy

CTA:

- schedule an introductory call on `www.recompfitnesslab.com`

Copy rule:

Keep claims descriptive and credible. Do not promise cures, disease prevention or guaranteed longevity outcomes.

### 6. Coach / Professional Layer

Purpose:

Show that RE-COMP is not only consumer tracking.

Content:

- coach dashboard
- user state overview
- progress monitoring
- action recommendations

This should evolve the old coaching offer into a more premium health guidance layer.

### 7. Final CTA

Purpose:

Close with clarity.

Content:

- short promise
- CTA
- product preview

## Visual Rules

- light-first
- minimal
- premium
- product visible early
- dark glass only for insight/data moments
- no generic fitness gradients
- no heavy decorative effects
- motion must explain the product
- premium wellness service sections may feel more editorial and location-led, but must stay credible and specific

## Prototype Stack

Recommended:

- React
- Vite
- GSAP ScrollTrigger
- Framer Motion
- Lenis
- Lucide React

Optional later:

- Lottie or Rive
- Three.js only if a real 3D health/product scene is needed

## First Prototype Scope

The first prototype should include:

- full page layout
- responsive desktop and mobile
- hero
- at least one scroll-based sticky section
- animated metric cards
- dark glass insight module
- product module section
- final CTA

It does not need:

- final copy
- final brand imagery
- backend integration
- full production optimization

## Developer Handoff Goal

Developers should be able to:

- run the prototype locally
- inspect components
- see animation logic
- reuse section structure
- understand the intended product direction
