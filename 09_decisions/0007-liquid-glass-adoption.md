# Decision 0007: Liquid Glass Adoption

## Status

Accepted.

## Decision

RE-COMP adopts the interaction principles and system material logic of Apple Liquid Glass where they improve native usability.

Liquid Glass is not the visual identity of RE-COMP.

## Use Liquid Glass For

- Navigation
- Controls
- Contextual actions
- Overlays
- Transient interaction surfaces
- AI Coach composer
- Temporary filters

## Do Not Use Liquid Glass As Default Material For

- Dashboard content
- Biomarker lists
- Plans
- Reports
- Tables
- Dense charts
- Long text
- All metric cards

## Reason

Liquid Glass can make the product feel native and premium, but overuse would weaken hierarchy and make RE-COMP feel like an Apple clone.

## Consequences

Core content surfaces must use RE-COMP's own design language:

- Clinical clarity
- Stable data presentation
- Proprietary typography
- Controlled color coding
- Modular health components
- Calm evidence-led hierarchy
