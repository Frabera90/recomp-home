# Decision 0001: Health OS Positioning

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

RE-COMP will be positioned as a Health Operating System / Human Optimization Platform.

It is not positioned as a generic fitness tracker, gym app or training log.

## Reason

The product direction is moving from training toward a broader health system that includes:

- wellness
- longevity
- performance
- recovery
- nutrition
- coaching
- AI guidance

## Consequences

- Product language must focus on health state, interpretation and guidance.
- UI should prioritize meaning and action, not raw tracking.
- Fitness features should support the larger health system, not dominate it.
- Marketing should communicate trust, intelligence and performance.
