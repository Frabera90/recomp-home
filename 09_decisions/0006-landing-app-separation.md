# Decision 0006: Landing And App UI Separation

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

The RE-COMP landing page and app UI will be treated as separate design surfaces.

They share:

- positioning
- brand values
- premium quality bar
- Health OS direction
- minimal, health-first tone

They do not share the same layout system or interaction model.

## Reason

The app must be operational, readable and useful every day.

The landing page must be narrative, persuasive and visually expressive enough to communicate the product vision.

## Consequences

- Landing references should not automatically change app UI rules.
- App UI references should not automatically define marketing layout.
- Landing may use richer motion, cinematic composition and scroll-based storytelling.
- App UI should remain task-oriented, structured and calm.
