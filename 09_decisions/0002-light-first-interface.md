# Decision 0002: Light-First Interface

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

RE-COMP will use a light-first interface.

White, off-white and very light neutral backgrounds are the default.

Dark surfaces are reserved for selected data, analytics, insight and premium biometric moments.

## Reason

The product needs to feel:

- trustworthy
- health-oriented
- premium
- clear
- medical-adjacent without being clinical

A light-first interface better supports the health and care positioning.

## Consequences

- Home remains primarily light.
- Dashboard remains primarily light.
- Dark glass cards may be used for high-value metrics.
- Recovery deep dives or analytical views may use darker surfaces.
- Full dark mode should not define the default product identity.
