# Decision 0005: Information Density

## Status

Proposed

## Decision

RE-COMP should use high-value information density without overwhelming the user.

## Reason

The product needs to feel serious and data-rich, but its value comes from interpretation.

Large numbers and biometric data should be paired with simple meaning and recommended action.

## Consequences

- Metric cards should not show numbers alone.
- Each major data surface should include interpretation.
- Dense dashboards are allowed when hierarchy is clear.
- The user should never need to decode the system alone.
