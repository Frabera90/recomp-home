# Decision 0003: Minimal Glassmorphism

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

RE-COMP will use glassmorphism only in a minimal, controlled way.

## Reason

The desired direction includes liquid glass and blurry glass effects, but the product must remain professional, calm and health-first.

Heavy effects would make the app feel decorative or generic AI-generated.

## Consequences

- Glass is used for cards, FABs, key data modules and selected overlays.
- Borders may have subtle brightness.
- Blur should be functional and restrained.
- Avoid decorative glow, excessive gradients and visual noise.
- Glass should support hierarchy, not become the visual concept itself.
