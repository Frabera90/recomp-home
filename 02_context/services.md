# RE-COMP Services

## Status

Working context from current RE-COMP service communication.

These services should inform product, landing, social, presentation and brand work, but must be phrased carefully. Do not turn them into unsupported medical claims.

## RECOMP Wellness Plan

RECOMP Wellness Plan is an advanced longevity and wellness program.

It offers personalized paths and exclusive locations in Italy for a premium wellness journey.

Current CTA:

- schedule an introductory call through `www.recompfitnesslab.com`

## Service Areas

### Biomarker Screening

- in-depth screening of key biomarkers
- baseline assessment for personalized wellness and longevity planning

### Longevity And Specialist Wellness Paths

- anti-inflammatory longevity-oriented infusion paths with specialist doctors
- specialist-supported wellness protocols

Important wording rule:

Do not present these as cures or guaranteed clinical outcomes unless approved medical/legal copy exists.

### Movement And Posture

- functional movement analysis
- posture assessment
- correction of imbalances, asymmetries and limitations
- recovery of movement efficiency, stability and quality

### Nervous System And Stress

- nervous system and stress state evaluation
- breathing techniques
- biofeedback
- autonomic regulation practices
- support for focus, resilience and mental well-being

### Metabolism, Nutrition And Body Composition

- advanced metabolic analysis
- body composition analysis
- personalized nutrition strategies
- personalized training strategies
- sustainable and measurable body optimization

## Product Implications

The app and product narrative should support a service ecosystem, not only a standalone tracker.

Important product concepts:

- assessment
- interpretation
- personalized plan
- human specialist layer
- daily guidance
- measurable progress
- premium wellness journey

## Brand Implications

RE-COMP should feel like:

- longevity and wellness intelligence
- premium health guidance
- specialist-supported optimization
- performance as care
- calm, credible, advanced Italian wellness

Avoid:

- generic fitness language
- exaggerated transformation claims
- medical certainty
- aggressive biohacking tone
- clinic-only coldness

## Landing Implications

The landing can introduce RECOMP Wellness Plan as a premium service layer connected to the Health OS.

Possible section framing:

- "From assessment to a personalized wellness plan"
- "A longevity program supported by specialists"
- "Biomarkers, movement, stress, metabolism and nutrition in one guided path"

## Social Implications

Social content can be organized around:

- biomarker literacy
- movement quality
- stress regulation
- recovery and longevity
- metabolic health
- sustainable body optimization
- behind-the-scenes premium wellness locations in Italy

Every social claim must remain educational or service-descriptive unless validated.

## Presentation Implications

Presentations should explain RE-COMP as both:

- a Health OS product direction
- a premium service ecosystem with assessment, specialists, guidance and measurable progress

## Claims Guardrail

Allowed language:

- supports
- helps guide
- evaluates
- assesses
- informs
- personalizes
- tracks progress
- wellness-oriented
- longevity-oriented

Avoid unless legally/clinically approved:

- cures
- treats
- reverses disease
- prevents disease
- guarantees longevity
- eliminates inflammation
- medical diagnosis
