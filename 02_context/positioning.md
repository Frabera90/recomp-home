# Positioning

RE-COMP sits between:

- Apple Health for system clarity
- Bevel for health intelligence
- Whoop for performance and recovery
- Superpower for medical wellness
- Nike for human performance energy

## Category

Health OS / Human Optimization Platform

Premium Longevity And Wellness Program

## Positioning Statement

RE-COMP helps people understand their body, improve their future and receive daily guidance across health, recovery, nutrition and performance.

Through RECOMP Wellness Plan, the brand also offers personalized longevity and wellness paths supported by biomarker screening, specialist services, movement analysis, stress regulation, metabolic assessment, nutrition and training strategy.

## Not

RE-COMP is not:

- a generic fitness tracker
- a gym app
- a training log system
- a pure medical app
- a dashboard full of disconnected numbers
- a generic biohacking program
- a generic wellness retreat

## Desired Perception

The product should feel:

- premium
- minimal
- credible
- calm
- intelligent
- performance-oriented
- health-first

## Strategic Territory

The brand lives between fit and medical.

It should make health feel advanced, personal and actionable without becoming cold or clinical.

## Service Positioning

RE-COMP should be positioned as a bridge between:

- digital health intelligence
- premium Italian wellness
- human specialist support
- performance and movement quality
- longevity-oriented prevention culture

The service layer should feel exclusive and personalized, but still credible, measurable and calm.
