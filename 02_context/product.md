# RE-COMP Product Definition

RE-COMP is evolving from a fitness and training app into a Health Operating System.

It brings together:

- wellness
- longevity
- performance
- recovery
- nutrition
- coaching
- AI health intelligence

It is also connected to RECOMP Wellness Plan, a premium longevity and wellness program built around personalized paths, specialist support and exclusive locations in Italy.

## Core Idea

Users do not simply track fitness.

They understand their current health state and receive clear guidance on what to do next.

## Product Shift

From:

- tracking data
- logging workouts
- displaying metrics

To:

- interpreting signals
- guiding behavior
- improving adherence
- connecting performance and care

## Product Principle

Every important product surface should help the user move from information to action.

The system should always produce:

- insight
- recommendation
- action

## Product Direction

RE-COMP should feel like a bridge between fitness, wellness and medical prevention.

It is not sport as discipline only.

It is sport, recovery and nutrition as care.

## Service Ecosystem

RECOMP Wellness Plan expands the product from an app-only idea into a guided wellness ecosystem.

Current service areas include:

- key biomarker screening
- longevity-oriented infusion paths with specialist doctors
- functional movement and posture analysis
- correction of imbalances, asymmetries and movement limitations
- recovery of movement efficiency, stability and quality
- nervous system and stress state evaluation
- breathing, biofeedback and autonomic regulation practices
- focus, resilience and mental well-being support
- advanced metabolic analysis and body composition
- personalized nutrition and training strategies
- sustainable and measurable body optimization

## Product Implication

The app should not feel like a disconnected tracker.

It should become the digital layer that helps users understand assessments, follow personalized plans, stay connected to guidance and measure progress over time.

## Claims Rule

Service copy must avoid unsupported medical claims.

Use careful language such as:

- assesses
- supports
- informs
- helps guide
- personalizes
- monitors progress

Avoid guaranteed outcomes unless validated and approved.
