# Product Goals

## Primary Goals

- simplify health complexity
- unify multiple health domains
- guide daily behavior
- increase adherence to healthy habits
- translate data into action
- support both users and professionals

## User Outcomes

The customer should be able to:

- understand their current body state
- know what matters today
- see progress over time
- receive practical guidance
- connect training, recovery, sleep and nutrition

## Business Outcomes

The system should support:

- customer engagement
- coaching workflows
- professional monitoring
- premium positioning
- franchise scalability

## Product Quality Bar

RE-COMP should feel like a coherent system, not a set of disconnected AI-generated screens.
