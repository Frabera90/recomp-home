# RE-COMP Design System Context

## Product Identity

RE-COMP is a Health Operating System.

It turns signals into understanding, understanding into guidance, and guidance into daily action.

The product sits between:

- fitness
- wellness
- recovery
- nutrition
- performance
- longevity
- coaching
- AI health intelligence

## Positioning

RE-COMP should feel like a bridge between fitness, wellness and medical prevention.

It is not:

- a generic fitness tracker
- a gym app
- a training log
- a pure medical app
- a dashboard full of disconnected numbers

Desired perception:

- premium
- minimal
- credible
- calm
- intelligent
- performance-oriented
- health-first

## Reference Translation

RE-COMP learns from:

- Apple: clarity, restraint, system elegance
- Bevel: health intelligence and biometric confidence
- Nike: performance energy and human movement
- Superpower: medical wellness seriousness
- Whoop: recovery/performance framing

But it must not imitate any of them.

The design system translates principles into a distinct RE-COMP language.

## Product Question

Every important app surface should help answer:

- How am I today?
- Why?
- What should I do next?
- Who or what is guiding that decision?

Every important marketing surface should communicate:

- RE-COMP is a Health OS.
- Data becomes guidance.
- Performance, recovery, nutrition, biomarkers and coaching are connected.
- The product is premium, credible and human.

## Non-Negotiables

- No generic fitness dashboards.
- No disconnected metric walls.
- No fake medical certainty.
- No unsupported medical claims.
- No decorative motion.
- No excessive glass.
- No Apple clone layouts.
- No AI chatbot aesthetic as the core identity.

## Design Priority

1. Clarity
2. Guidance
3. Data legibility
4. Native iOS behavior
5. Restrained Liquid Glass

## Content Principle

Scores and metrics must include context and a next action.

Preferred:

`Recovery is stable. A moderate strength session fits today.`

Avoid:

`Crush your goals.`

## Current Strategic Decisions

- Landing and app are separate runnable projects.
- The design system is the shared layer between them, not a place for product logic.
- Liquid Glass is accepted for interaction surfaces, not as the RE-COMP identity.
- App UI pilot is provisional until reconciled with the original app and Claude redesign code audit.
- Landing can be more cinematic and narrative than the app.
- App must stay operational, readable and useful every day.
