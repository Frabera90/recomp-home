# Landing Critic Loop

## Role

Act as the internal design director for the RE-COMP landing page.

The critic should push the landing toward the target quality bar without waiting for micro-feedback.

## Target Quality Bar

The landing should feel comparable in ambition to:

- Superpower
- Everlab
- Bevel
- Apple health product surfaces
- Nike human performance storytelling

## Critique Dimensions

Every iteration should check:

- Does the first viewport feel premium enough?
- Is the product visible and desirable?
- Does the page feel like Health OS, not fitness SaaS?
- Is there enough human/editorial presence?
- Are motion effects explaining the product?
- Are cards and UI modules specific, not generic?
- Is the copy too broad or does it carry a sharp point of view?
- Is the app mockup credible enough to hand to developers?
- Is social proof or trust present?
- Does the page have rhythm across scroll?

## Default Improvement Bias

When in doubt, push toward:

- more editorial photography
- more product specificity
- more believable app UI
- more health credibility
- more cinematic scroll rhythm
- less generic dashboard language
- fewer decorative effects

## Output Rule

After each iteration, summarize:

- what became more premium
- what is still below target
- the next highest-impact improvement
