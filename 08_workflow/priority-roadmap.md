# RE-COMP Priority Roadmap

## Status

Working roadmap for coordination, priorities, stakeholder updates and delivery checkpoints.

This document is owned by System Architect.

## Current Strategic Update

RE-COMP must not be perceived as another AI food/training tracker.

The key differentiation is:

- AI guidance
- tracked data
- real professionals behind the experience
- paid specialist reviews and consultations
- service layer connected to the digital Health OS

The product should make clear that AI and professionals guide the user together.

## Strategic Message To Preserve

RE-COMP is a premium luxury-tech wellness and longevity platform where data, AI and professionals work together to guide the user's path.

## Product Differentiators

### 1. AI + Professional Guidance

RE-COMP should show that users are not alone with an algorithm.

The product must expose:

- AI guidance
- professional review
- specialist interaction
- paid consultations
- analysis booking

### 2. Bookable Professional Services

Priority features and surfaces:

- book analysis
- specialist review
- consultation with professional
- paid specialist session
- review request after tracked data or food/training input

### 3. Food Tracker As Near-Term Investor Signal

Food tracker has become a near-term technical priority.

Reason:

- promised modification by end of August
- useful to keep investors engaged
- connects nutrition data to AI/professional guidance
- helps prove RE-COMP is moving beyond visual redesign
