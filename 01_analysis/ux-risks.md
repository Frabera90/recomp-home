# UX Risks

## Feature Overload

RE-COMP risks becoming too broad if health, training, nutrition, recovery, biomarkers and coaching are all presented with equal weight.

Mitigation:

Define clear user states and daily priorities.

## Unclear Primary Navigation

If navigation mirrors feature categories too literally, users may not know where to start.

Mitigation:

Prioritize Today, Health State and Actions before deep data sections.

## Fitness and Medical Confusion

The product sits between performance and care. Without hierarchy, it may feel either too clinical or too gym-focused.

Mitigation:

Use health interpretation as the central layer.

## Generic AI Experience

A chatbot interface could weaken the premium Health OS positioning.

Mitigation:

AI should appear as guidance, insight and decision support across flows.

## Inconsistent Visual System

Mixing Apple, Bevel, Whoop, Superpower and Nike references without rules could create visual noise.

Mitigation:

Keep white minimal surfaces as the base and use dark glass only for selected data moments.
