# Old RE-COMP Website Audit

Source:

- `https://www.recompfitness.com/`

## How To Treat This Source

The current public website is historical source material.

It should be used to understand:

- existing offer
- existing business model
- current product language
- current feature areas
- legacy positioning

It should not define the new Health OS direction.

## Current Positioning Observed

The site currently positions RE-COMP as a personalized coaching and fitness app.

Key language:

- fitness journey
- healthier you
- personalized coaching app
- nutrition strategy
- workout tracking
- coach support
- free body check

## Current Offer Areas

The site is structured around:

- Nutrition
- Training
- Coaching
- Packages
- Free Body Check
- Business / Professional / Owner

## What To Preserve

### Free Body Check

This is a strong bridge into the Health OS direction.

It can evolve from a lead-generation fitness check into:

- health assessment
- baseline body state
- first step into the Health OS
- conversion point for membership

### Coaching

The coach relationship is strategically valuable.

It can evolve from fitness coaching into:

- human guidance
- professional oversight
- care layer
- health interpretation support

### Nutrition

Nutrition remains important, but it should move from macro tracking to health guidance.

### Training

Training remains important, but it should be framed as part of health, recovery and longevity.

### Business / Professional Layer

The old site already suggests a B2B or operator layer.

This supports the existing system map:

- Customer System
- Coach System
- Franchise / Operator System

## What To Move Away From

### Generic Fitness Language

Phrases like fitness journey, achieve your fitness goals and healthier you are too generic for the new positioning.

### App Feature Listing

The old site lists nutrition, workout tracking and coaching as separate features.

The new landing should show these as one connected operating system.

### Package-First Framing

Pricing and coaching packages should not lead the first impression.

The new first impression should be:

- intelligence
- health state
- guidance
- trust
- performance and care

### Transformation Copy

Transformation language should be reduced unless it is made more precise and health-oriented.

## Strategic Translation

Old:

Personalized coaching app for fitness, nutrition and training.

New:

Health OS that connects testing, recovery, nutrition, training and coaching into daily guidance.

## Landing Implications

The new landing should:

- preserve body check as a conversion mechanic
- preserve coaching as a trust layer
- preserve nutrition/training as modules
- reposition everything under health intelligence
- introduce biomarkers, recovery and longevity
- feel less like a gym app and more like a premium health platform

## Copy Direction

Avoid:

- Elevate your fitness journey
- Achieve your fitness goals
- Start your change now
- Be part of the community

Prefer:

- Understand your body
- Turn health data into a daily plan
- Start with your baseline
- Connect performance, recovery and care
- Know what to do next
