# RE-COMP Presentation Story Structure

## Status

Draft scaffold. Waiting for user recap before final narrative decisions.

## First Deck Goal

Define the clearest version of what RE-COMP is, why it matters, how it works and why the current product direction is different from a generic wellness or fitness app.

## Initial Narrative Arc

1. Problem: health and performance data are fragmented, noisy and hard to act on.
2. Shift: users do not need more tracking; they need interpretation, guidance and continuity.
3. RE-COMP: a health operating system that translates signals into recovery, nutrition, biomarkers and coaching decisions.
4. Product proof: app flows, dashboards, modules and guidance surfaces.
5. Brand proof: calm precision, premium intelligence, restrained Liquid Glass and Soft Metal material language.
6. Experience proof: landing, social, generated imagery and visual system.
7. Roadmap: what is validated, what is provisional and what needs product/code audit before build decisions.
