# RE-COMP Presentations

## Purpose

This folder is the workspace for RE-COMP decks and presentation planning.

It should define what we say, in what order, and with which visuals before generating final slides.

## Source Of Truth

Before working on a presentation, read:

- `RECOMP_MASTER.md`
- `VISUAL_LANGUAGE.md`
- `02_context/product.md`
- `02_context/positioning.md`
- `02_context/product_philosophy.md`
- `brand/README.md`
- `brand/brand-guidelines.md`
- `design-system/tokens.css`
- `05_marketing/landing-blueprint.md`
- `05_marketing/social/README.md`
- `06_assets/generated/ASSET_LOG.md`

## Presentation Files

- `story-structure.md`: narrative spine and slide sequence.
- `layout-direction.md`: presentation layout rules.
- `asset-map.md`: selected screenshots, logos, mockups and image sources.
- `deck-backlog.md`: future presentation tasks and open questions.

## Visual Sources

Presentations can use:

- app screenshots and app pilot references
- landing screenshots and landing assets
- logo and brand assets
- generated images and mockups
- social visuals and campaign language
- current product screenshots from `00_input/screenshots/current_recomp/`

## Rules

- Do not invent medical claims.
- Do not exaggerate product behavior.
- Mark assumptions explicitly.
- Prefer real app, landing and generated visuals over generic placeholders.
- Keep the story precise, premium and calm.
