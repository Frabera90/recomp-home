# RE-COMP App UI Pilot Brief

## Status

Provisional.

## Objective

Create a UI pilot that validates the RE-COMP app direction without pretending to define the final product architecture.

This pilot should show the team the desired quality level and clarify how RE-COMP uses native-aware interaction, restrained Liquid Glass and proprietary health intelligence surfaces.

## Scope

Included:

- Home / Daily Overview
- Recovery Detail
- AI Coach
- Foundational components required by those screens

Excluded:

- Final onboarding
- Coach dashboard
- Clinician flows
- Franchise flows
- Admin flows
- Production data model
- Medical decision logic
- Definitive route structure

## Design Direction

- Light-first app experience.
- Dark immersive surfaces only for focused diagnostic moments.
- Liquid Glass mainly for navigation, controls and overlays.
- Stable content surfaces for health data.
- One primary decision per screen.
- Calm clinical voice.

## Validation Questions

- Does the interface answer "How am I today and what should I do?"
- Does Health Score feel like a decision surface, not just a number?
- Does Recovery Detail explain why the score exists?
- Does AI Coach feel contextual rather than generic?
- Does Liquid Glass support interaction rather than dominate the visual identity?
