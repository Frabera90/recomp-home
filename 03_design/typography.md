# RE-COMP Typography Rules

Typography should feel premium, calm and highly readable.

## Scale

- Hero: 64+
- Section headline: 48
- Screen title: 34-40
- Card headline: 24-28
- Body: 16-18
- Caption: 12-14

## Rules

- Do not scale font size directly with viewport width.
- Letter spacing should usually be 0.
- Use uppercase sparingly for labels, eyebrows and system metadata.
- Health numbers should be large, clear and confident.
- Long health explanations should use comfortable line height.

## Tone

Headlines should be direct and calm.

Avoid generic motivational phrasing.
