# RE-COMP UI Anti-Patterns

Avoid these patterns unless explicitly justified.

## Product Anti-Patterns

- Dashboards with many equal-weight widgets.
- Raw data without interpretation.
- Multiple competing recommendations.
- Repeated CTAs.
- Screens that do not answer a clear user question.
- Flows shortened by removing necessary states.

## Visual Anti-Patterns

- Cards for everything.
- Centering every layout.
- Neon gradients.
- Decorative orbs.
- Oversized shadows.
- Fake 3D.
- Excessive glass.
- Busy transparency over dense data.
- Apple clone navigation or tab bars.

## Motion Anti-Patterns

- Animating every scroll.
- Delaying access to information.
- Bouncy or playful transitions.
- Motion that looks impressive but explains nothing.

## Reference Anti-Patterns

- Do not imitate Apple.
- Do not imitate Whoop.
- Do not imitate Superpower.
- Do not imitate Bevel.

Understand why they work, then translate the principle into RE-COMP.
