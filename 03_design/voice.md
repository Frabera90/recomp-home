# RE-COMP Voice

RE-COMP speaks with calm confidence.

It is precise, human and grounded.

## Voice Principles

- Clinical without being cold.
- Encouraging without hype.
- Direct without sounding robotic.
- Actionable without overclaiming.
- Calm even when something needs attention.

## Avoid

- "Optimize your wellness journey."
- "Crush your goals."
- "Unlock your full potential."
- "Excellent work!"
- "Your body is hacked."
- Generic AI assistant language.

## Prefer

Instead of "Optimize your recovery":

- "Recovery is improving."

Instead of "Excellent work!":

- "You're recovering well."

Instead of "Boost your performance":

- "Today's load can increase safely."

Instead of "You need to improve your sleep":

- "Sleep is the first lever today."

## Recommendation Pattern

Use:

1. Current state
2. Reason
3. Suggested action

Example:

"Recovery is stable. HRV and sleep are both trending up, so a moderate strength session fits today."
