# RE-COMP Product Constitution

This document governs product and UI decisions across RE-COMP.

## Core Belief

RE-COMP is a Health Operating System.

It turns signals into understanding, understanding into guidance, and guidance into daily action.

## Decision Rules

1. Do not design isolated screens. Every screen must belong to a flow.
2. Every screen must answer one clear user question.
3. Every new feature must prove why it exists.
4. Information hierarchy is more important than visual effect.
5. Liquid Glass is a material, not the RE-COMP identity.
6. Motion must explain the product, not decorate it.
7. When uncertain, choose clarity, legibility and trust over spectacle.
8. Health data must never create unnecessary anxiety.
9. Recommendations must be contextual, calm and actionable.
10. The product must feel native-aware, not Apple-branded.

## Design North Star

The interface should help the user understand:

- How am I today?
- Why?
- What should I do next?
- Who or what is guiding that decision?

## Non-Negotiables

- No generic fitness dashboards.
- No disconnected metric walls.
- No fake medical certainty.
- No decorative motion.
- No excessive glass.
- No Apple clone layouts.
- No AI chatbot aesthetic as the core product identity.
