# RE-COMP Design Direction

## Design Position

RE-COMP should sit between Apple, Bevel and Nike:

- Apple: clarity, restraint, system elegance
- Bevel: intelligent health dashboards and premium biometric depth
- Nike: human performance, movement and confidence
- Superpower: medical wellness, longevity and serious health orientation

## Visual Principle

Minimal white surfaces with dark glass data modules.

The main experience should feel light, clean and controlled, while selected data, insights and high-value moments can use darker blurry glass cards.

## Backgrounds

Default background:

- white
- off-white
- very light cool gray

Special sections may use:

- dark panels
- glass overlays
- high contrast data modules

Avoid full-screen darkness as the default product tone.

## Glass Treatment

Use glassmorphism with restraint.

Preferred:

- subtle blur
- translucent dark cards
- fine borders
- minimal highlights
- controlled reflections

Avoid:

- heavy gradients
- decorative orbs
- excessive glow
- busy transparency
- futuristic noise

## Data Style

Numbers should be large, clear and confident.

Data modules should prioritize:

- one dominant metric
- one short interpretation
- one recommended action

Do not present raw data without meaning.

## Color Direction

Core palette:

- white
- soft gray
- graphite
- black glass
- electric blue accents
- health green accents
- occasional warm recovery or nutrition accents

Use accent colors sparingly and functionally.

## Typography

Typography should be:

- modern
- clean
- neutral
- highly readable

Headlines should feel premium and calm, not loud or motivational in a generic fitness way.

## UI Mood

The product should feel:

- premium
- exact
- calm
- intelligent
- health-oriented
- athletic without being aggressive

## Product Screens

Primary screens should avoid marketing-style layouts.

They should feel like a working health interface:

- dense enough to be useful
- calm enough to be trusted
- structured enough to scale

## Design Rule

RE-COMP should never look like a normal fitness app with a health skin.

It should look like a health intelligence system that understands performance.
